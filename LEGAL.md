# Legal & Ethical Use Notice

This notice covers the Media Downloader suite (the "Universal Downloader"
tool). It is the standalone counterpart of the notice shown in-app the
first time you open the downloader, and of the summary in
[README.md](README.md#legal--ethical-use-media-suite). It is short,
factual, and not legal advice.

## The short version

LocalTools is built the same way, and is legal to build and distribute,
as other long-established open-source downloader projects (`yt-dlp`,
`youtube-dl`, `gallery-dl`). It does not circumvent DRM and only downloads
content already served to any visitor's browser.

## Terms of Service vs. copyright law

Many platforms' Terms of Service restrict downloading content even when
there's no technical DRM preventing it. Using this tool may violate the
ToS of the site you're downloading from — separately from copyright law
itself. That's on you to consider; the app can't adjudicate it for you.

## Appropriate uses (recommended, non-exhaustive)

- Downloading **your own uploaded content** (e.g., backing up your own
  channel or profile media).
- Content **explicitly licensed for reuse/download** — Creative Commons,
  public domain, or anything the rights-holder offers for download.
- **Personal offline viewing** of content you have the right to access.
- **Archival, fair-use, and educational purposes** consistent with your
  local copyright law.

## What LocalTools will never do

The app provides no feature specifically designed to strip DRM, bypass
paywalls or logins, or facilitate bulk redistribution or re-hosting of
downloaded content — and none will be added. Sites that `yt-dlp` doesn't
recognize are rejected with a clear "unsupported site" error rather than
attempting any raw-fetch fallback.

## Jurisdiction caveat

Copyright law and platform Terms of Service vary by country and by
platform. This notice is factual information, not legal advice — you are
responsible for complying with both the law and the ToS wherever you use
LocalTools.
